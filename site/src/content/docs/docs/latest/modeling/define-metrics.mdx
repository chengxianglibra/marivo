## Build one complete definition

For `ms.measure_column`, `name=` is the semantic name and `column=` selects the
physical field; `additivity` is required and `unit` is optional. Derived Measures
and Metrics take their names from the decorated function. Their zero-argument
bodies compose typed dependencies, which the loader binds before publishing the
Catalog. See the [complete model example](/docs/latest/modeling/semantic-layer/#complete-model).

## Preserve the business rule

Units describe a quantity; additivity specifies where summing is permitted.
Choose both from business meaning, since numeric type alone does not grant sum
permission. Set time, null, and sample policies on the corresponding Metric
operators. See [Preserve the business rule](/docs/latest/modeling/semantic-layer/#preserve-the-business-rule).
