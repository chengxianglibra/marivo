Choose how source dates or timestamps are interpreted, which time field supplies
business time, and an explicit observation range. The range includes its start
and excludes its end. Treat the source parser timezone, calendar boundary
timezone, and `Session` reporting timezone as separate roles. Define business
periods with an appropriate calendar and boundary timezone; model activities or
holidays that can overlap as named occurrences. Workday counts need a schedule
covering each selected date, including compensatory workdays; a missing status is
unknown. Strict sample reducers check expected clock points only when a
`TimeDimension` declares `sample_interval` and the observation range is finite. See
[Date and time definitions](/docs/latest/modeling/dates/) and
[Time series and windows](/docs/latest/analysis/time-series/).
