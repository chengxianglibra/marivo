The harness or business data service maintains business calendars. Marivo
executes analysis using ordinary business fields and explicit time ranges.

- Fiscal grouping: model dates and fiscal keys, then aggregate the original Metric by the business dimension.
- Holiday comparison: the harness chooses exact bounds and pairing, then constructs `session.time_scope(...)`.
- Working-day filters: use ordinary date data and working status; retain unknown states and select non-contiguous members explicitly.
- Natural windows: use `mv.grain(...)`, `scope.by(grain)` and supported window rules.

See [dates and time](/docs/latest/modeling/dates/) for source encoding and timezone
declarations, and [time series](/docs/latest/analysis/time-series/) for bucketing and windows.
