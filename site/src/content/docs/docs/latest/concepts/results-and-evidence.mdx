A completed `AnalysisFrame` holds the values produced by its evaluation. A saved
Frame also carries the audit record for that result. For source-backed results,
read it as the outcome of captured source reads and scope, not a live view of
current data.

## Inspect the saved result

On a saved Frame, `show()` gives a bounded preview; `show(n=0)` displays metadata
and audit summaries. `to_pandas()` exports all fixed values as a flat table.
Keep the Frame for typed analysis. To reopen the exact result in another
process, use its `session_id` and `artifact_id`.

The [results guide](/docs/latest/analysis/results/#reopen-the-saved-data) shows
how to save and reopen a result, and how to export its values.

## Read evidence in context

Saved audit records separate source reads and scope (when applicable), the
evaluation and its checks, and the committed Session and Artifact.
`evidence_digest()` reads the saved Evidence facts; `findings()` reads Findings
attached to the commit. An ordinary observation can have source and evaluation
facts without producing Findings.

Source-read, evaluation-completion, and commit times are distinct. Use the
recorded source scope and read time to judge freshness; a recent commit alone
does not establish fresh source data. To analyze current source values, capture
a new observation.

After a successful supported `attribute()` analysis, the Frame exposes
`method_facts` that explain how the selected method decomposes an output within
its recorded scope and assumptions. They describe the analysis method, not the
cause behind an observed value or change. See
[how to read evidence](/docs/latest/analysis/results/#read-evidence-in-context).
