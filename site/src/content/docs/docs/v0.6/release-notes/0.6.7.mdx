Released on November 13, 2026. Marivo 0.6.7 improves Catalog discovery and
diagnostics, and offers clearer guidance for time-partitioned analysis.

## Highlights

- When datasource declaration files fail to execute,
  `ms.load(allow_partial=True)` can retain a validated Catalog subset for
  browsing. Search reports an incomplete scope; `catalog.load_errors` and
  `catalog.excluded_refs` explain the failure and omitted definitions. Readiness,
  validation, preview and analysis Sessions still require a strict load.

## Changed

- `print(catalog.search())` displays a bounded result card, including datasource
  identities for displayed Entities. The typed `page.items` retains the complete
  current page, and `next_cursor` continues pagination.
- Semantic check diagnostics identify authored locations when available and show
  concise errors by default. Pass `--traceback` for full Python traces.

## Fixed

- Partial loading isolates failed datasource imports and excludes unavailable
  definitions and their dependents while preserving structural, dependency and
  identity validation.
- Observation errors for DATE and TimeDimension inputs can suggest declared
  `hour_prefix` fields for explicit source-clock selection. Changing a report
  grain does not silently replace a Metric's contribution clock.

## Documentation

- Added guidance for safe time-partition modeling and filtering, including the
  effect of changing clocks on membership and totals.
- Linked read Help directly to its members and synchronized the English and
  Chinese v0.6 guides with the current documentation.
